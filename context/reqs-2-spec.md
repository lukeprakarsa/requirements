#### 5 AI[🔗](#(part._reqs-2-ai) "Link to here") 

You are welcome to use AI for this assignment. Specifically, you may now
use Claude Code.

Keep in mind that you take responsibility for the work it does! (If you
work at a company and you produce code that causes problems for the
company, they’re not going to fire Claude, they’re going to fire you.)
You should understand its output and be able to answer questions about
it. Treat it as an aid to your work, not as a substitute for your work.
